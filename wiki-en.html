GETTING STARTED

Getting started

  1. Download the client from the official website. Use the version announced by the team and update before logging in. An outdated-client message means you need an update, not a new account.
  2. Create your character on the login screen. Keep your username and password safe. Never send your password in chat or to other players.
  3. Get to know your party. Heroes, also called hirelings, have their own health, attributes and equipment. Your character on the world map represents the party.
  4. Start with creatures and the daily quest. Type /diaria to check your target and read the messages after each battle.
  5. Prepare your inventory. Check equipment, materials and free slots before crafting or collecting rewards.

Item and class names are kept exactly as they appear in the game. Chat commands remain unchanged in both languages.

PARTY AND PROGRESSION

Heroes and evolution

Each hero progresses individually. Health (HP) determines how much damage a hero can take; mana (MP), when used by the class, is a resource for abilities. Physical and magical damage and defense are separate attributes.

03

UNDERSTAND COMBAT

Battles, PvE and PvP

PvE: against creatures

Fight monsters to progress and complete objectives. The daily quest checks recorded creature kills from completed battles; check your progress afterward.

PvP: against players

Your opponent is another player and their hirelings. Being on a PvP map or killing a monster is not the same as defeating a player.

Before attacking

  • Check who owns the selected unit and which heroes are still alive.
  • Check each unit's health, resources and position before choosing an action.
  • Follow the client's indicators for turns, movement, range and valid targets.
  • Do not confuse one hireling's death with the defeat of an entire party.

The weekly system described here has no automatic 2v2 matchmaking. It tracks player defeats in the existing combat system and uses individual scores.

04

ITEMS AND MATERIALS

Crafting and enchanting

Use the client's Craft, Enchant +2 and Enchant +3 menus. Select a recipe and check the resulting item, required materials and quantities before crafting.

  1. Keep the materials in your inventory and leave at least one free slot.
  2. Check the full equipment name, including its level and +2 or +3 suffix.
  3. Consider the risk: materials are deducted before the success roll.
  4. Wait for the success or failure message before trying again.
ACTUAL RECIPE EXAMPLE

Wizard Staff 1 +3: 1 × Wizard Staff 1 +2 and 4 × Enchant Stone 1. This recipe's configured success chance is 50%.

Do not apply this percentage to all crafting: each recipe defines its own chance. Failure may consume materials and the base equipment according to the recipe; do not expect an automatic refund.

50% does not guarantee one success every two attempts. Each attempt is rolled individually. Unequipping other items does not change the recipe's configured percentage.

06

COMPANIONS

Collectible pets

Pets are cosmetic companions that follow your character on the map. Equipping a pet does not grant attack, defense or experience bonuses in the system described here.

  1. Type /pets to see the pets you own, not every pet in the game.
  2. If Cat is unlocked on your account, use /pet Cat to equip it.
  3. Use /petoff to put your companion away. This does not remove the unlock.

07

CLANS AND FORTRESSES

Guilds and the Fort

The Clan menu contains guild functions. The configured limit is 3 total members, including the leader. Lord and General can complete invitations according to the system's permissions.

  • The invited player must accept; entering their name alone is not enough.
  • A guild with three members needs a free slot before admitting another player.
  • Use clan communication and functions to organize your activities.

Howl Fortress A — x800y800

The Fort map keeps its original player and guild PvP rankings. The weekly coins ranking is separate: it has its own rules and does not replace the Fort's historical standings.

A Fort “PvP Points” message does not guarantee that the fight counted for the week. Check /pvpstats and /pvprank for the weekly event.

08

COMPETITIVE EVENT

Weekly PvP arena

AVAILABLE WHEN THE TEAM OPENS THE EVENT

This guide explains the arena configuration. It is not a live indicator of availability, schedule or results. Check in-game announcements.

This is not a raffle: placement is based on points from valid fights against players on x800y800 — Howl Fortress A.

  1. Use /arena to reach the map when entry is open. Players already on the map can also score.
  2. Fight with another player as your opponent. The Fort's zGoblin is a creature: killing it alone does not earn a weekly win.
  3. A win is recorded when the opposing player's last hireling is defeated, not for every fallen unit.
  4. Check /pvpstats and /pvprank. Use /sairarena outside battle; returning requires a position previously saved by the entry command.

To receive a reward, you need a positive score and at least one win. The maximum individual prize is 100 coins. Ties retain the existing ranking order.

End of the week

The week runs Monday through Sunday using the VPS calendar. The team pays rewards manually at the announced closing time; there is no automatic scheduled payment. Results are frozen for payout. If payment is pending, the previous week's results are preserved before another week opens. Follow the announced server time rather than assuming your local US time zone.

This configuration has no winning-streak bonus, exclusive title or weekly guild rewards. Players sharing an internet connection may share an IP and have their fights excluded from scoring.

ECONOMY

Titan Coins, Gold and VIP

Gold and Titan Coins (TC) are different currencies. Check your premium balance with /coins and spending options with /lojacoins.

PurchaseCostCommand
7 days of VIP10 TC/vip7
15 days of VIP20 TC/vip15

These commands make purchases; they are not just information requests. In /comprargold 5, the number means spending 5 TC to receive 4,250 Gold. Check your balance before sending it.

The configured VIP benefit is 30% additional battle experience. Purchasing more days extends the existing expiration date if it is still active. Premium currency is stored separately from normal inventory items.

Buying through the website

  1. Open the official store and correctly enter your character name and the requested email.
  2. Check the package and price before paying. The website offers 5, 10, 30 and 50 TC packages. Prices are in Brazilian reais (BRL), not USD, and checkout currently uses Brazil's PIX system.
  3. PIX approval and game synchronization are separate steps. Follow the store's instructions and log into the game again to check your balance.
  4. If coins do not arrive after confirmation and synchronization, contact the team with your payment ID. Do not pay again to try to “unstick” delivery.

QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

I won a battle but received no weekly points.

Check that the event is open, the battle took place on x800y800 and another player's last hireling was defeated. Same IP, unverified IP and defeating the same victim within 10 minutes prevent scoring.

My pet is unlocked but does not appear.

Check /pets, equip it with /pet Cat and use the updated client. If the message confirms the pet, send the team a screenshot and your client version; visual assets may be missing.

Why does crafting not start?

Check for inventory space and make sure materials, quantities and equipment level match the recipe exactly. Keep the error message to report to the team.

The website says online, but I cannot log in.

The website checks whether the server port responds. This does not guarantee authentication, the correct client version or every game feature working. Check your version, credentials and maintenance announcements.

How do I report a problem?

Include your character name, time, map, action taken and error message. Send a screenshot without passwords, access codes or personal payment details.
